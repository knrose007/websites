Orlando & Walt Disney World

Everything you need to plan a magical, low-stress visit: when to go, how to get there, which parks to see, and what to pack.

Getting there

Walt Disney World is in Lake Buena Vista, about 20 miles southwest of downtown Orlando. Most visitors fly into Orlando International Airport (MCO), about a 25–30 minute drive away.

✈️

Fly into MCO

Orlando International has nonstop flights from most U.S. cities. Sanford (SFB) is a smaller option about 45 minutes farther north.

🚐

Airport to hotel

Rideshare and taxis are the easiest. Shuttle services run between the airport and Disney hotels, and a rental car makes sense if you plan to explore beyond Disney.

🚝

Getting around

Disney's free buses, boats, the Monorail, and the Skyliner gondola connect the parks and resort hotels, so you may not need a car at all.

When to go

Orlando is warm year-round. Crowds follow school calendars, so timing your trip around holidays makes the biggest difference.

Quieter times

Mid-January through February (outside holiday weekends), and early November until the Thanksgiving rush.

Hot but calmer

Late August and September. Lines are shorter, but expect heat, humidity, and the peak of hurricane season.

Busiest

Christmas to New Year's, spring break (March–April), and summer. Plan ahead and arrive early.

Summer afternoons often bring short thunderstorms, so plan indoor shows or a hotel break around 2–5 p.m.

The four theme parks

Each park has its own personality. With four days you can give each one a full day; with fewer, pick the ones that match your group.

Magic Kingdom

The classic. Castles, parades, and fireworks.

  • Walk down Main Street, U.S.A. to Cinderella Castle
  • Classic rides in Fantasyland and Adventureland
  • Stay for the nighttime fireworks show

EPCOT

Science, discovery, and food from around the world.

  • Ride inside Spaceship Earth, the giant sphere
  • Stroll World Showcase's 11 country pavilions
  • Seasonal festivals for food, flowers, and art

Hollywood Studios

Step into the movies.

  • Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge and Toy Story Land
  • Big thrills like the Tower of Terror
  • Live stage shows throughout the day

Animal Kingdom

Wildlife, adventure, and lush landscapes.

  • See real animals on Kilimanjaro Safaris
  • Explore the glowing world of Pandora
  • Go early. It often closes before the other parks.

Have extra time? Disney also has two water parks (Typhoon Lagoon and Blizzard Beach) and Disney Springs, a free-to-enter area for shopping, dining, and entertainment.

Where to stay

🏨

Disney resort hotels

Value, Moderate, and Deluxe tiers. Perks include Early Theme Park Entry and free transportation to every park.

🚡

Skyliner resorts

Several hotels sit on the Skyliner gondola line, giving an easy, scenic ride to EPCOT and Hollywood Studios.

💰

Nearby hotels

Lake Buena Vista and International Drive have lots of hotels and vacation rentals, often cheaper. Budget for parking or rideshares.

A sample five-day trip

A relaxed pace with one park per day and a rest day in the middle.

Magic Kingdom

Arrive before opening, ride the classics, take a midday break, and come back for fireworks.

Animal Kingdom

Start with the safari while animals are most active, then see Pandora. Have an early dinner and get a good night's sleep.

Rest & Disney Springs

Sleep in, enjoy the hotel pool, then shop and eat at Disney Springs in the evening.

Hollywood Studios

Head straight to the most popular rides at opening, then fill the afternoon with shows.

EPCOT

Do the rides in the morning, then "eat around the world" in World Showcase as the sun goes down.

Tips that save time

📱

Use the app

The My Disney Experience app shows wait times and maps, and lets you mobile-order food so you skip the lines.

⏰

Go early

The first hour after opening has the shortest lines of the day. Arriving early is the best free strategy there is.

⚡

Consider Lightning Lane

Disney's paid skip-the-line options can save hours on busy days. Check current options and prices before you go.

🍽️

Book dining early

Popular sit-down restaurants and character meals fill up fast. Reservations open weeks ahead.

Packing list

  • Comfortable, broken-in walking shoes
  • Sunscreen and a hat
  • Refillable water bottle
  • Rain poncho for afternoon storms
  • Portable phone charger
  • Sunglasses
  • A light layer for air-conditioned rides
  • Small backpack or crossbody bag
  • Ziploc bag to keep your phone dry
  • Band-aids and blister pads
